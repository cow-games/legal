Crown Thieves Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Crown Thieves (Android package com.emtv.heist) handles information. The game is published on Google Play under the developer name CRAZY COW by ENGAGE MEDIA TV LTD ("we", "us").

In short: Crown Thieves has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. There is no multiplayer, no chat, no advertising and no in-app purchases. To understand how the game is played, it uses one third-party service, Google Analytics for Firebase, which receives device and usage information such as an app instance identifier, your device's advertising ID where available, and your IP address.

Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress and settings: the best stars you have earned on each mission and whether sound is muted. These are kept in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Information collected by third-party services

The game includes the following Google Firebase software development kits (SDKs). They collect information directly from your device under Google's privacy policies.

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: used to understand how the game is played, for example missions started, won, lost or abandoned, the stars earned, whether an alarm was raised, gold collected, time taken, guards taken down, pockets picked, locks picked, items used (coins, water and crossbow bolts) and screens viewed. Along with these events, Firebase collects an app instance identifier, your device's advertising ID where available, device and app information (such as model, operating system version, language and app version) and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging: part of the game's Firebase setup, which lets us send occasional notifications about the game. Crown Thieves does not currently ask for permission to show notifications, and Android shows none without that permission. The SDK may register a Firebase installation identifier and a messaging token with Google.

Apart from the SDKs listed above, the game does not use any advertising, attribution, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

How information is used

Information collected through these services is used only to understand how the game is played, to find missions that are too hard or too easy, and to fix problems and improve the game. We do not sell your personal information, we do not use it for advertising, and we don't use it for anything unrelated to running and improving the game.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, analytics relies on our legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played and keeping it working well. You can object at any time (see "Your choices and rights" below).

Sharing

Information is shared only with Google, which provides Firebase to us as a service provider. We do not share it with advertising networks or data brokers. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

Data retention and security

Information collected by Google is kept according to its own retention policies; user-level analytics data in Google Analytics is kept for no longer than 14 months. All communication between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device). Analytics then no longer receives it.
  • Play offline: the game plays fully without an internet connection. Analytics events recorded while you are offline may be sent the next time your device connects.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the game stops all further collection and removes the data stored on your device.
  • Access and deletion: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we don't hold accounts or names, we may need your device's advertising ID or app instance identifier to find data held for you. Email us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will handle the request, including deleting analytics data linked to that identifier, within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Crown Thieves is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data about them has been collected, contact us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will delete it.

Third-party privacy policies

  • How Google uses information from apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
  • Privacy and Security in Firebase: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the game or the services it uses change. We'll post the new version on this page and change the "Last updated" date above.
