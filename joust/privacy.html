Knight's Lance: Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains what information is collected when you play Knight's Lance (the "game"), a mobile game published on Google Play by ENGAGE MEDIA TV LTD ("we", "us", "our"), and how it is used and shared.

The game has no accounts and no logins. We never ask for your email address, phone number, photos, contacts or precise location. There are no in-app purchases, no chat and no way to share content with other players.

Information collected

  • Game progress and settings (your knight's name and coat of arms, gold, gear, tournaments won, sound on or off) are stored only on your device. We do not receive them. The knight's name is whatever you type in the game; it never leaves your device.
  • Gameplay analytics. We use Google Analytics for Firebase to understand how the game is played: for example knights created, tournaments started and finished, matches won or lost, gear bought, gold earned and rewarded ads watched. Along with these events, Firebase collects an app instance identifier, your device's advertising ID where available, device and app information (device model, operating system version, language, app version) and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.
  • Advertising. The game shows rewarded ads only: an ad plays only when you choose to watch one (to plead for a rematch after a lost match, or to double a tournament's winnings). There are no banner or interstitial ads. Ads are delivered by Google AdMob and by the Engage Ads SDK, our own ad technology. To choose, deliver and measure ads and to prevent fraud, ad requests include your advertising ID where available, your IP address (used to estimate approximate location), device and app information (such as device model, operating system version, language and app version), ad interaction data (such as whether an ad was shown, clicked or watched to the end) and, where applicable, your consent choices. AdMob and our ad server may share this information with advertising partners that bid to show an ad.
  • Install attribution. We use Tenjin to learn which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install of the game. The Tenjin SDK collects your advertising ID, device and app information, your IP address and the Google Play install referrer. It also receives a small number of in-game events (tournament started, tournament finished, rewarded ad watched) and ad revenue information. Tenjin may pass install and event information to the advertising network that showed you an ad for the game, so that network can measure and optimize its campaigns.

The game does not use any other analytics or crash-reporting service besides the ones listed above, and it does not send push notifications.

How we use information

  • To understand how the game is played, fix problems and improve tournament design and difficulty.
  • To show and measure the rewarded ads you choose to watch, and to prevent ad fraud.
  • To measure which of our advertising campaigns bring players to the game, and how far those players get.

We do not sell your personal information for money. We do not use the information to identify you by name.

Consent and legal bases

If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form the first time it runs, and ads are personalized only if you agree. Your consent choices are passed to our ad partners. Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, we rely on your consent for personalized advertising, and on our legitimate interests for analytics, non-personalized advertising, attribution and fraud prevention.

Third-party services

These providers process information under their own privacy policies:

  • Google (AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and the User Messaging Platform consent form): Google Privacy Policy, How Google uses information from apps that use its services and Privacy and Security in Firebase.
  • Tenjin (install attribution): Tenjin Privacy Policy.
  • Engage Ads (ad delivery): operated by ENGAGE MEDIA TV LTD under this policy.

Data retention

Analytics data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above; user-level analytics data in Google Analytics is kept for no longer than 14 months. Ad and attribution data is kept only as long as needed for the purposes above: by Google under its own retention policies, by Tenjin for as long as needed to report on campaigns, and by our ad server for as long as needed to deliver, measure and bill ads and to prevent fraud. Data stored on your device is removed when you uninstall the game.

International transfers

We are based in Israel, and our service providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, transfers are protected by appropriate safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

Security

All information the game sends is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we and our providers use reasonable measures to protect it.

Your choices and rights

  • Ads are optional: you never have to watch one to play the game.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete it, or opt out of personalized ads, in your device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads).
  • Stop all collection: uninstall the game.
  • Access, correction, deletion and objection: depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK, Israel or US states such as California), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information about you, to object to or restrict its processing, to withdraw consent, and to opt out of targeted advertising. To make a request, including a request to delete your data, email info@engagemedia.tv. Because the game has no accounts, please include your device's advertising ID if you can, so we can find the relevant data. We will respond within the time required by law, and we will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.
  • You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13 (or the higher minimum age that applies in your country). If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, contact us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will delete it.
