Siege Breaker Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Siege Breaker (Android package com.emtv.breaker) handles information. The game is published on Google Play under the developer name CRAZY COW by ENGAGE MEDIA TV LTD ("we", "us").

In short: Siege Breaker has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. To show optional rewarded ads and to measure where installs come from, the game uses Google AdMob, our own Engage Ads SDK and Tenjin. These services receive device and usage information such as your advertising ID and IP address.

Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress and settings: the battles you have unlocked, your stars and gold, weapon and army upgrades, and your sound setting. These are kept in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Information collected by third-party services and our ad SDK

The game includes the following software development kits (SDKs). They collect information directly from your device.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform: used to show rewarded video ads, which play only when you tap a button to watch one (for fresh troops after a defeat, or double gold after a victory), and to ask for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, your IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), your interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data. Google uses this to deliver, measure and personalise ads, to prevent fraud and to improve its services, under its own privacy policy. Where consent is required, you choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Engage Ads SDK: our own advertising software, which can also serve the same optional rewarded ads. When it requests an ad it sends device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), your IP address, the advertising ID where available, and your consent choices to our ad server, which may pass parts of this information in bid requests to advertising partners so they can offer an ad. It respects the consent choices you make in the consent form.
  • Tenjin: a mobile attribution service that tells us which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install, and how players progress and engage with ads. Tenjin collects your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as model, operating system, language, app version and install referrer), app open events, when a battle starts and ends, when you finish a rewarded ad, and ad revenue information for ads shown in the game. Tenjin may share install and event data with the advertising networks we use to promote the game (such as AppLovin) so they can measure and optimise our campaigns.

The game does not use any other analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files. It has no in-app purchases and no chat or other way to interact with other players.

How information is used

Information collected through these services is used to show and measure ads, to work out which of our marketing campaigns bring players, to prevent fraud, and to keep the game free to play. We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for anything unrelated to running and promoting the game.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising and the related use of identifiers rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent form shown by the game and can change later from the Privacy settings button on the title screen. Attribution, fraud prevention and non-personalised ads rely on our legitimate interest in measuring our marketing and keeping the game free and secure.

Sharing

Information is shared only as described above: with Google (AdMob), with advertising partners through our Engage Ads SDK, and with Tenjin and, through Tenjin, the advertising networks that run our campaigns. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

Data retention and security

Information collected by Google and Tenjin is kept according to their own retention policies. Ad request data received by our ad server is kept only as long as needed to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud and meet legal obligations. All communication between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Consent: where consent is required, you can change your choice at any time from the Privacy settings button on the title screen.
  • Ads are optional: an ad only plays when you tap a "Watch an ad" button. You can play the whole game without watching any.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the game stops all further collection and removes the data stored on your device.
  • Access and deletion: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we don't hold accounts or names, we may need your device's advertising ID to find data held for you. Email us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will handle the request, including asking Tenjin to delete data linked to that identifier, within 30 days. You can also contact Google and Tenjin directly using the links below, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Siege Breaker is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data about them has been collected, contact us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will work with our partners to delete it.

Third-party privacy policies

  • Google (AdMob, User Messaging Platform): https://policies.google.com/privacy
  • Tenjin: https://tenjin.com/privacy/

Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be reflected in the game's Google Play listing.
