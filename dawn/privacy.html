Dawnbringers Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Dawnbringers (Android package com.emtv.dawn) handles information. The game is published on Google Play under the developer name CRAZY COW by ENGAGE MEDIA TV LTD ("we", "us").

In short: Dawnbringers has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or contacts. Your progress stays on your device. To show optional rewarded ads, understand how the game is played and measure where installs come from, the game uses three third-party services: Google AdMob, Google Analytics for Firebase and Tenjin. They receive device and usage information such as your advertising ID and IP address.

Information stored only on your device

  • Game progress and settings: your world, camp and items, the hero name, colour and class you pick, and the co-op server address if you enter one. These are kept in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Local co-op: if you choose to play co-op, the game connects to a relay server on your own local network (Wi-Fi), usually run by one of the players. Your hero name, class, colour, position and in-game actions are shared with the other players in that session through that relay. We don't run that relay and receive none of this data. There is no chat and no online matchmaking.

Information collected by third-party services

The game includes the following software development kits (SDKs). They collect information directly from your device under their own privacy policies.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform: used to show rewarded video ads, which play only when you tap the button to watch one, and to ask for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data. Google uses this to deliver, measure and personalise ads, to prevent fraud, and to improve its services. Where consent is required, you choose whether ads may be personalised.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: used to understand how the game is played, for example nights survived, raids fought, quests completed, camp upgrades built, rewarded ads watched and which screens of the game you view. Along with these events, Firebase collects an app instance identifier, your device's advertising ID where available, device and app information (such as model, operating system version, language and app version) and an approximate location (country or region) derived from your IP address.
  • Tenjin: a mobile attribution service that tells us which advertising campaign, if any, led to an install, and how those players engage with ads. Tenjin collects your advertising ID, IP address, device and app information (such as model, operating system, language, app version and install referrer), app open events, the event that you finished a rewarded ad, and ad revenue information. Tenjin may share install and event data with the advertising networks we use to promote the game (such as AppLovin) so they can measure and optimise our campaigns.

Apart from the SDKs listed above, the game does not use any analytics, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs, and it does not request access to your precise location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

How information is used

Information collected through these services is used to understand how the game is played, fix problems and improve it, to show and measure ads, to work out which of our marketing campaigns bring players, to prevent fraud, and to keep the game free to play. We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for anything unrelated to running and promoting the game.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising and the related use of identifiers rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent form shown by the game and can change later. Analytics, attribution, fraud prevention and non-personalised ads rely on our legitimate interest in understanding how the game is played, measuring our marketing and keeping the game free and secure.

Sharing

Information is shared only as described above: with Google (AdMob and Google Analytics for Firebase) and Tenjin, and through Tenjin with the advertising networks that run our campaigns. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

Data retention and security

Information collected by Google and Tenjin is kept according to their own retention policies; user-level analytics data in Google Analytics is kept for no longer than 14 months. All communication between the game and these services is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

Your choices and rights

  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Consent: where consent is required, you can change your choice in the consent form the game shows.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the game stops all further collection and removes the data stored on your device.
  • Access and deletion: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. Because we don't hold accounts or names, we may need your device's advertising ID to find data held for you by our partners. Email us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will handle the request, including asking Tenjin to delete data linked to that identifier, within 30 days. You can also contact Google and Tenjin directly using the links below, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Dawnbringers is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data about them has been collected, contact us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will work with our partners to delete it.
