Monkey Mimic Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains how the mobile game Monkey Mimic (Android package com.emtv.mimic) handles information. The game is published on Google Play under the developer name CRAZY COW by ENGAGE MEDIA TV LTD ("we", "us").

In short: Monkey Mimic uses your phone's front camera so you can copy the monkey's poses. The camera picture is analysed on your device and is never uploaded to us or anyone else. The snapshots it takes stay on your phone unless you choose to send one. There are no accounts. To show optional rewarded ads, the game uses Google AdMob, which receives device information such as your advertising ID and IP address.

Camera and photos

  • Camera: the game asks for camera access to play. While you play, a pose and face tracker (Google MediaPipe, bundled inside the app) looks at the camera picture to find the positions of your head, face, arms and hands and compare them with the monkey's pose. This happens entirely on your device, frame by frame. The camera picture and the tracking data are not sent to us or to any third party, and they are not used to identify you.
  • Snapshots: at the end of each pose the game takes a snapshot and turns it into a picture with the monkey and your score. The last 15 snapshots are kept in the app's local storage so you can see them in the album. They are not uploaded, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.
  • Sharing: a picture leaves your phone only when you tap SEND and pick an app in Android's share menu. It then goes to the app and people you chose, under that app's own privacy policy. We never receive it.
  • The game does not use the microphone, and it does not read your photo gallery, files, contacts or precise location.

Other information stored only on your device

  • Game progress and settings: your best scores, your bananas (the plays you have in stock and when the next one grows back) and your sound setting. These are kept in the app's local storage. They are not sent to us, and they are deleted when you uninstall the game or clear its data.

Information collected by third-party services

The game includes the following software development kit (SDK). It collects information directly from your device under its own privacy policy.

  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) and Google User Messaging Platform: used to show rewarded video ads, which play only when you tap the button to watch one (to get a banana right away), and to ask for consent where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland). AdMob may collect your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, IP address (which can indicate your approximate location), device and app information (such as model, operating system, language and app version), interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data. Google uses this to deliver, measure and personalise ads, to prevent fraud, and to improve its services. Where consent is required, you choose whether ads may be personalised. AdMob never receives your camera picture or snapshots.

Apart from AdMob, the game does not use any analytics, attribution, crash-reporting or tracking SDKs.

How information is used

Information collected by AdMob is used to show and measure ads, to prevent fraud, and to keep the game free to play. We do not sell your personal information, and we don't use it for anything unrelated to running the game.

Legal basis (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

Where the GDPR or UK GDPR applies, personalised advertising and the related use of identifiers rely on your consent, which you give or refuse in the consent form shown by the game and can change later with the PRIVACY button on the title screen. Fraud prevention and non-personalised ads rely on our legitimate interest in keeping the game free and secure.

Sharing

Information is shared only as described above: with Google through AdMob, and with the apps you pick when you send a picture yourself. We may also disclose information if the law requires it or to protect our rights and users.

Data retention and security

Information collected by Google is kept according to Google's own retention policies. All communication between the game and AdMob is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Camera pictures are not kept at all, apart from the snapshots in the on-device album described above.

Your choices and rights

  • Camera: you can turn camera access off at any time in your Android settings (Settings > Apps > Monkey Mimic > Permissions). The game can't be played without it.
  • Advertising ID: you can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android device settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads, or Settings > Google > Ads, depending on your device).
  • Consent: where consent is required, you can change your choice with the PRIVACY button on the title screen.
  • Stop all collection: uninstalling the game stops all further collection and removes everything stored on your device, including the snapshots.
  • Access and deletion: depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act), you may have the right to access, correct or delete personal information, or to object to its processing. We don't hold accounts, names, pictures or other data about you ourselves. Email us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will help within 30 days. You can also contact Google directly using the links below, and you have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Children

Monkey Mimic is not directed to children under 13 (or the higher minimum age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has used the game and data about them has been collected, contact us at info@engagemedia.tv and we will work with Google to delete it.
